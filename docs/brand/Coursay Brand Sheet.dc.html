01 · Name

Course, the path you follow, fused with say, because you say your reasoning out loud. Pronounced cour‑SAY. Always lowercase in the wordmark; “Coursay” in running text.

02 · Mark

A hub with six spokes: five open nodes and one filled teal node, where you are now. It is the Learning Path node map reduced to a glyph, so the symbol and the roadmap share a language. Strokes are 2 units at 48; nodes are 2.5, the hub 3.5, the current node 5.

03 · Wordmark

Syne Bold, tracking −0.04em. Never stretched, never re‑set in another face. Wordmark height equals the mark’s node span; the gap between them is one third of the mark width.

04 · App icons and favicon

32 · 24 · 16 — small sizes drop the open nodes, thicken strokes to 3.2
